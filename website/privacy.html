1. Introduction & Commitment

At Meri Local Bazaar ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize user data confidentiality, transaction security, and local community trust. This Privacy Policy sets forth our policies and procedures on the collection, usage, processing, and protection of your personal information across our Android mobile app and services.

2. Information & Data We Collect

To deliver seamless local buying, selling, cab bookings, and repair services in Meghalaya and neighboring Northeast districts, we collect the following limited data points:

  • User Account Details: Name, WhatsApp / Calling Phone Number, Primary Location / City / District, and optional Email Address.
  • Classified Listing Content: Advertisement titles, detailed item or service descriptions (e.g. cab routes, electronics rates, room rentals), asking price, and user-uploaded media/photos.
  • PRO Membership Payment Verification Data: The official 12-digit UPI UTR / Transaction Reference ID submitted by users to verify manual GPay / PhonePe / Paytm payments for PRO listing boosts.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Android OS version, device model, IP address, and non-identifiable app crash diagnostics.
🛍️ Visual Marketplace: Data is utilized solely to connect local users across Mobiles, Bikes, Cabs/Taxi, and Property categories.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob SDK Integration

Meri Local Bazaar integrates Google AdMob (by Google LLC) to display contextual banner ads and rewarded video ads (used by sellers to unlock the 1 Month Free PRO Active Plan):

  • Google AdMob may collect and process standard technical identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, and anonymous performance signals.
  • These identifiers enable Google to deliver contextual, non-intrusive ads that comply with standard Google Publisher & Developer Policies.
  • Users can easily reset or limit ad personalization at any time through their Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. How We Use & Process Collected Data

  • Hyperlocal Discovery: To index and display relevant nearby products, cab drivers, and technicians to buyers in your district.
  • PRO Verification: To manually cross-check submitted 12-digit UTR numbers against admin transaction records to activate listing badges.
  • Direct Connection: To allow local customers to initiate direct WhatsApp chats or phone calls without platform middlemen.
  • Anti-Fraud & Safety: To monitor, detect, and suspend prohibited ads, fake accounts, duplicate listings, or scam attempts.

5. Data Sharing & Public Visibility

Public Information: By publishing an ad on Meri Local Bazaar, you understand that your seller name, phone number, listing photos, item description, and town/locality are made publicly visible to allow interested local buyers to connect with you.

We Never Sell Your Data: Meri Local Bazaar does NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal phone numbers, emails, or UTR logs to any third-party marketing companies or data brokers.

6. User Control: Editing & Deleting Your Data

  • Listing Edits & Removal: You can edit or delete any of your active ads and photos directly from your app dashboard whenever you choose.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account, seller profile, or transaction history, simply email us at merilocalbazaar@gmail.com with your registered contact number. Requests are processed within 7 business days.
